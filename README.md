# Bembung - The Ultimate Ring Stacking Challenge!

Get ready for a fun and fast-paced arcade experience! In Bembung, your goal is simple: push the rings onto the stick before time runs out. But don’t be fooled—precision and speed are key!

🔹 Fast-Paced Gameplay – Race against the clock to stack the rings.

🔹 Challenging Levels – The difficulty increases as you progress.

🔹 Simple Yet Addictive – Easy to play, but hard to master!

🔹 Compete & Improve – Beat your best time and climb the leaderboard.

Can you master the perfect timing and complete all the challenges? Download Bembung now and test your skills! 🎯🔥
